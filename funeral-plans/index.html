Planning ahead

Two separate things: writing down what you want, and paying for it in advance. The first is free, and it is the one that helps your family most.

People come in to talk about this for all sorts of reasons — a diagnosis, a friend's funeral that went badly, or simply reaching an age where it feels sensible. Whatever brought you here, there is no pressure and there never will be.

Start with your wishes, not your wallet

You can come in and record what you would like for your funeral without paying us anything at all. Burial or cremation, which church or crematorium, hymns, readings, who should be told, whether you want flowers or donations, what you absolutely do not want.

We keep it on file, and we will give you a copy to keep with your will. It is not a legally binding document. What it is, is an enormous relief to the people left behind, who otherwise spend the worst week of their lives guessing.

This costs nothing

Recording your wishes with us is free, carries no obligation to buy anything, and does not commit your family to using us. Come in whenever suits. 01368 863636.

Pre-paid funeral plans

A funeral plan lets you pay for your funeral now, at today's prices. We arrange plans provided by Golden Charter, who work with around 3,000 independent funeral directors across the UK and have been doing so for more than 35 years.

What a plan covers

The funeral director's services are guaranteed — our part will be covered whatever it costs by the time it is needed, however far in the future that is. Third-party costs such as the crematorium fee, the burial fee and the minister's fee work differently: depending on the plan, these are covered by an allowance which may or may not meet the full cost at the time.

That distinction is the single most important thing to understand about any funeral plan, from any provider. We will go through exactly what your plan does and does not guarantee before you sign anything, and we will put it in writing.

Paying

  • A single payment
  • Twelve monthly instalments, interest-free
  • Spread over a longer period, up to 15 years, where interest applies

Your money is protected

Since July 2022, pre-paid funeral plans have been regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority — a significant improvement on the position before, when the sector was largely unregulated and some providers behaved badly.

Golden Charter Limited is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (Firm Reference Number 965279). You can check this on the Financial Services Register at register.fca.org.uk.

Your money is held in a trust that is separate from us. We never hold it. If we were no longer able to carry out your funeral for any reason, Golden Charter would arrange another funeral director from their network.

What we earn from this

We receive a commission from Golden Charter when we arrange a plan. You should know that, and you should factor it into how you weigh our advice — which is why we have put it here rather than in the small print.

It is also why we would rather you started with the free wishes conversation. If a plan is right for you, it will still be right after you have thought about it.

Is a plan right for you?

It may suit you if

  • You want the certainty that it is dealt with and paid for
  • You have strong views about your funeral and want them followed
  • You would rather your family did not have to find several thousand pounds
  • You expect to stay in this area, so the same funeral director will carry it out

It may not, if

  • You have savings you could simply set aside instead — money in an account remains yours and stays flexible
  • You are likely to move a long way, though plans can transfer
  • You might qualify for Funeral Support Payment — a fully paid plan substantially reduces what your family can claim

We will tell you honestly if we think a plan is not the right answer for you. It happens, and we would rather say so.

How to start

Come in, or telephone, and we will spend as long as you want on it. Bring your partner, or an adult child, or come alone. Nothing is decided on the day, and you will leave with everything in writing.
