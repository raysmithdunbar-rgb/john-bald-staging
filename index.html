The funerals we arrange

Anything is possible, so long as it keeps the dignity of the person who has died. If what you want is not listed here, ask us anyway.

  • Burial

    In your local churchyard or cemetery across East Lothian and Berwickshire.

  • Cremation

    With a full service at the crematorium, a church, or here at our funeral home.

  • Direct cremation

    A simple cremation with no service, at a time of our arranging.

  • Natural burial

    Woodland and green burial, with fully biodegradable coffins.

  • Children's funerals

    There is no charge for burial or cremation for a child in Scotland.

  • Repatriation

    Bringing someone home, from anywhere, or taking them onward.

Independent, and staying that way

John Bald opened these doors in Dunbar over fifty years ago. When he retired, the business passed to Ian and Elizabeth Torrance, who ran it with the same care for another twenty years. In 2022 it came to us — Awdri and Allan Doyle — because Ian wanted it to stay in independent hands rather than go to one of the national chains.

Bruce Robertson manages the funeral home and looks after families here day to day. Three sets of hands have held this business in fifty years, and the one thing that has never changed is how it is run — independently, from Dunbar, answering its own telephone.
