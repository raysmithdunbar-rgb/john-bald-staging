Traditional Wooden Coffins

Material
Wood
Suitable for
Burial, Cremation
Biodegradable
Yes, fully
Price
Please ask — we will tell you straight away

Not every family wants a woven or cardboard coffin, and there is no reason they should. We carry the full conventional range alongside the eco coffins.

Veneered coffins are the most commonly chosen: a wood veneer over a board core, in oak, mahogany, elm or walnut finish, with a choice of handles and a fitted lining. Solid wood coffins in oak or elm are also available and cost more.

Both are suitable for burial and cremation. If you would like a traditional appearance with a lower environmental impact, ask us about the printed woodgrain cardboard coffin — at a distance most people cannot tell.

To choose this coffin

Telephone 01368 863636 or send us a message. We will confirm the price and arrange it for you — coffins are supplied through funeral directors rather than bought directly.
