Coffins

The full Ecoffins range, alongside traditional wooden coffins. Have a look before you come in, so it is not a decision made cold across a desk.

Choosing a coffin is one of the few decisions in a funeral that is genuinely about taste rather than logistics, and it is the one families most often feel rushed through. These pages are here so you can think about it in your own time.

We supply the complete range from Ecoffins (opens in a new tab), who have been making handwoven natural coffins for over twenty years, as well as conventional wooden coffins. Every coffin here is suitable for cremation as well as burial unless the page says otherwise.

How to order one

Coffins are supplied through us rather than bought directly — Ecoffins sell only to funeral directors, which is true of every coffin maker in the UK. Tell us which one you would like and we will arrange it, usually within two working days.

Telephone 01368 863636 or send us a message and we will tell you the cost of any coffin here, with no obligation.

A few things worth knowing

  • Eco does not mean flimsy. Every coffin here is tested to carry safely and is used routinely for burial and cremation.
  • You can use a natural coffin anywhere. You do not need a natural burial ground to choose willow or cardboard — they are perfectly usual in an ordinary cemetery or crematorium.
  • Price varies more than you would expect. Cardboard is the least expensive by some margin; solid hardwood the most. The woven coffins sit between.
  • You may buy a coffin elsewhere. If you would rather source your own, or make one, we will use it. Just tell us, and we will check the dimensions with the crematorium or cemetery beforehand.
