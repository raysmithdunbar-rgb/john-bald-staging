Coming to see us

You do not need an appointment to call in and ask a question. Plenty of people put their head round the door to ask about a funeral plan, or to record what they would like for themselves, or simply because they have been meaning to for months. The kettle is always on.

If you are coming to arrange a funeral, or to spend time with someone in our care, telephone first so we can make sure the room is free and that you are not disturbed.

Visiting someone in our care

Whenever you would like, for as long as you would like — including evenings and weekends. There is no limit on how many times you come. Children are welcome if you feel it would help them, and we will talk you through what to expect beforehand.

Services here

We can hold a short service at the funeral home for a small gathering. For some families it is exactly right — quieter than a church, warmer than a crematorium chapel, and no travelling. Ask us if you would like to see the room.

Parking and access

There is parking at the funeral home, and the building is accessible at ground level. If anyone in your family has particular needs — mobility, a wheelchair, a hearing loop, or simply somewhere quiet to sit away from other people — tell us in advance and we will have it ready.
