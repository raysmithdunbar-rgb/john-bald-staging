Crematoria, cemeteries and registrars

Most of what families need to know is not really about funerals. It is about which office, which fee, and who to telephone. So we wrote it all down.

This directory covers the whole of the east of Scotland, not only the area we serve. If you are arranging a funeral in Edinburgh or the Borders with a different funeral director, the information here is still yours to use — we would rather it existed somewhere than that families kept having to telephone round to find it.

  • Crematoria

    6 entries

    Every crematorium serving East Lothian, the Borders and Edinburgh — the chapel, the capacity, the parking, whether they stream services, and what the place is actually like.

  • Cemeteries and burial grounds

    6 entries

    Council burial grounds and churchyards across East Lothian and Berwickshire, with who manages each one, how to enquire about a lair, and the memorial rules that apply.

  • Registration offices

    4 entries

    Where to register a death across East Lothian, Midlothian, Edinburgh and the Borders, with booking arrangements and what to take with you.

Kept current, and dated so you can tell

Every entry carries the date it was last checked. Fees, opening hours and facilities change — often each April — and a directory that does not say when it was written is worse than no directory at all. If you find something out of date, please tell us and we will correct it.
