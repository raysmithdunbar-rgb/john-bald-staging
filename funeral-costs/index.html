What a funeral costs

You should never have to ask twice what something costs, or find out at the end.

Money is the thing families are most reluctant to raise and most anxious about. So we publish everything, we say it out loud before you ask, and we give you a written estimate before you commit to anything at all.

The lists

Standardised Price List

The headline prices for an attended and an unattended funeral, in the format every UK funeral director is required to use. This is the one to compare against other funeral directors, because the format is fixed and the same things are included.

Additional Options Price List

Everything else you might choose — coffins, cars, flowers, notices, memorials — itemised. All of it optional.

Crematorium, cemetery and celebrant fees

What the crematoria, councils and celebrants near Dunbar charge. These are set by them, not by us, and we pass them on at cost. Listed here so you can see the whole cost.

Help with funeral costs

Funeral Support Payment and other help available in Scotland. We will help you apply, and once you have, it can be paid directly to us so the money never has to pass through your hands.

One thing to watch when you compare

Always ask whether a quoted price includes the crematorium or burial fee. Some advertised prices are the funeral director's own charges only, with several hundred pounds of third-party fees added afterwards. Our lists make plain which is which, and you are entitled to that clarity from anyone you ask.

What drives the cost

  • Burial or cremation. Burial usually costs more, mainly because of the lair purchase and interment fee, both of which are council charges rather than ours.
  • Whether anyone attends. A direct cremation costs considerably less than an attended funeral.
  • The coffin. The range is wide. Recycled cardboard is the least expensive, solid hardwood the most, with the woven eco coffins in between.
  • Cars, flowers and printing. All optional, and easily the part families most often decide they do not need.

Paying

Our invoice is normally due within 30 days of the funeral. Where the estate is settling it, we will invoice the estate and wait — banks will usually release funds for a funeral bill before anything else is resolved. If you need longer, ask. We would rather agree something workable than have a family avoid the telephone.
