Standardised Price List

Published in the format every funeral director in the UK must use, so that you can compare us against anyone else, line for line.

The Competition and Markets Authority requires every funeral director in the United Kingdom to publish the list below, using its wording, its items and its order. That is deliberate: it means you can put our list beside anyone else's and compare them properly, rather than trying to work out whether two different quotes cover the same things.

Because the format is fixed by law, the list itself is worded by the CMA rather than by us. Our own explanation of what it all means follows underneath it.

Standardised Price List

All funeral directors are legally required to publish this Price List for a standardised set of products and services. This is to help you think through your options and make choices, and to let you compare prices between different funeral directors (because prices can vary).

What this means in practice

You are not obliged to buy anything else

You may buy only the attended or unattended funeral above, and nothing more. You are under no obligation to take any additional item we offer, and we will not press you to. Everything else we can supply is set out separately on our Additional Options Price List.

Fees we pay on your behalf

The "fees you must pay" above are set by other people — councils, crematoria, ministers — not by us. We pay them for you and pass them on exactly as charged, with nothing added. The two largest are the burial fee, set by East Lothian Council or Scottish Borders Council, and the cremation fee, set by the crematorium. Neither is charged for anyone under 18 in Scotland.

The footnote about doctors' fees applies in England, Wales and Northern Ireland. It does not apply here — Scotland has a different system, and there is no doctors' fee to pay for a cremation.

Help with paying

If money is a worry, please say so at the start rather than at the end. Funeral Support Payment is available in Scotland and can be paid straight to us, so it never has to pass through your hands. More about help with funeral costs.
