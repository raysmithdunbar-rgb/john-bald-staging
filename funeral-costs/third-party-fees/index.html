Crematorium, cemetery and celebrant fees

These fees are set by other people, not by us. We list them so you can see the whole cost before you decide.

We pass these on at cost

Everything on this page is charged by the crematorium, the council or the celebrant. We pay them on your behalf and add nothing. Our own charges are on the Standardised Price List and the Additional Options Price List.

Each list shows the date its owner gave it. Fees change, usually every April, so please treat this page as a guide and ask us for the figure that applies to your funeral.
