East Lothian Crematorium sits just north of Old Craighall, a short distance from Musselburgh, and is the crematorium most East Lothian families use. It is operated by Westerleigh Group.

## Getting there from Dunbar

About 22 miles by the A1 — normally around forty minutes, though the approach to Musselburgh slows at peak times. Allow extra if the funeral is mid-morning or late afternoon.

Parking is generous and the approach is level, which matters if anyone in the family has difficulty walking. [Houndwood](/directory/crematoria/houndwood-crematorium/) in Berwickshire is closer to Dunbar, but it sits on a hill in a 180-year-old church and is difficult for anyone with mobility problems — which is why many Dunbar families come here instead.

## The chapel

Bright and modern, with a lot of natural light and views out to the gardens. Families who have found older crematoria oppressive very often remark on the difference.

## The immersive system

This is what makes it unusual. It is the only crematorium in Scotland with a full immersive audiovisual system — imagery can be projected across the walls of the chapel. A hillside they walked. A coastline. A football ground. Photographs at a scale that changes how a room feels.

Written down it can sound like a gimmick, and for some families it is emphatically not the right thing. Used with restraint it can be remarkable. You are under no obligation to use it at all, and nobody will suggest the funeral is lacking without it.

## Time

Crematoria allocate fixed slots. That is enough for a full service with music, readings and a eulogy, but it is a real limit and worth planning around.

If you want longer, you can usually book a double slot. Alternatively, hold the main service at a church or at the funeral home and come here afterwards for a short committal — plenty of East Lothian families do exactly that.

## Streaming

Services can be streamed for family unable to travel, and recordings are usually available afterwards. If relatives are abroad, say so early so it can be set up properly.

## The gardens

The memorial gardens are extensive and well maintained, with plaques, benches and rose bushes available. Ashes can be interred or scattered here, and the gardens are open to visit outside service times.

## Afterwards

Ashes are normally ready within a few days. Your funeral director will collect them and hold them for as long as you need — there is no deadline and usually no charge.
