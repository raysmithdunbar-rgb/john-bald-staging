East Lothian Council's registration service operates from **John Muir House, Brewery Park, Haddington EH41 3HA**, on 01620 827308.

Appointments are required. They are usually available within two or three days, so the eight-day deadline is rarely a problem in practice.

## You do not have to register in East Lothian

In Scotland a death may be registered at any registration office in the country. If you live in Edinburgh, or the Borders, or Fife, and it is easier to register near home than to travel to Haddington, you may.

This surprises most people, and it saves a good deal of driving. See our [directory of registration offices](/directory/registrars/) for the alternatives.

## What to take

The Medical Certificate of Cause of Death is now usually sent to the registrar electronically, so you may not need to carry anything. Otherwise, take what you can find of:

- The person's birth certificate
- Their marriage or civil partnership certificate
- Their National Insurance number
- Any pension or benefit details
- Your own photo identification

If some of it cannot be found, go anyway. Missing paperwork very rarely stops a registration.

## What you will be asked

Full name and any previous names. Date and place of birth. Occupation. Usual address. Marital status. And the names and occupations of both parents — **including the mother's maiden surname**.

That last one is the question that catches people out, particularly for an elderly parent whose own mother died decades ago. Ask a relative before you go.

## Who may register

A relative, someone present at the death, the executor or solicitor, the occupier of the building where the death happened, or anyone else who knows the details and is taking responsibility for the funeral.

## What you come away with

- **The Certificate of Registration of Death (Form 14).** Free. This is what the funeral director needs before the funeral can go ahead.
- **An abbreviated extract.** Free.
- **Full extracts.** Chargeable. These are the death certificates banks, pension providers and insurers ask for.

## How many extracts to buy

More than you think. Each bank, each pension, each insurer, each share registrar will want its own — and many will not return it. Most families need somewhere between four and eight.

They cost less bought at the appointment than ordered later, so err upward.

## Tell Us Once

The registrar will offer it, and it is worth the extra ten minutes. It notifies HMRC, the Department for Work and Pensions, the Passport Office, DVLA and East Lothian Council's own services in a single step.

It does **not** cover banks, private pensions, insurance companies or utilities. Those you still have to do yourself.

## If the Procurator Fiscal is involved

You cannot register until the Fiscal releases the death. The eight-day clock effectively pauses, and you will not be penalised for a delay that is not yours. The Fiscal's office will tell you when you can proceed.
