Families in Berwickshire — Eyemouth, Duns, Coldstream, Chirnside and the surrounding villages — register with Scottish Borders Council rather than East Lothian.

## Booking

Appointments are required. The council operates registration from several locations across the Borders, and which offices are open on which days has changed over recent years, so telephone or check the council website before travelling rather than assuming your nearest is staffed.

## What to take

The same as anywhere in Scotland: the person's birth certificate, marriage or civil partnership certificate, National Insurance number and pension details if you can find them, plus your own photo identification.

## Remember

A death may be registered at any registration office in Scotland. If Haddington is more convenient than a Borders office, you may use it — and the reverse is equally true.
