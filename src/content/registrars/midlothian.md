Midlothian Council registers deaths for Dalkeith, Bonnyrigg, Gorebridge, Newtongrange, Penicuik and the surrounding area.

## Booking

An appointment with a registrar is required for any death registered in Scotland. Contact Midlothian Council by telephone or email to arrange one.

## Remember

As anywhere in Scotland, you may register at any office in the country. Midlothian, East Lothian and Edinburgh appointments are all equally valid regardless of where the person lived or died.
