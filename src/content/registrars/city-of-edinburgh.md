Many East Lothian families find themselves in Edinburgh when someone dies, because the Royal Infirmary and the other Lothian hospitals serve this area.

## When you might use it

If the person died in an Edinburgh hospital and you are in the city anyway, registering there can save a journey. It is also worth checking Edinburgh's availability if Haddington cannot see you quickly — you are free to use either.

## Booking

Appointments are required and are booked through the council. Edinburgh is busier than East Lothian, so book as early as you can.

## Remember

Registering in Edinburgh does not affect where the funeral can be held, or which crematorium or cemetery you use. It is purely an administrative step.
