East Lothian Council's registration service operates from John Muir House in Haddington, about eleven miles from Dunbar.

## Booking

Appointments are required — you cannot turn up on the day. Book by telephone on 01620 827308, by email, or through the council's online booking service. Appointments are normally available within two or three days, so the eight-day deadline is rarely a difficulty.

## What to take

The Medical Certificate of Cause of Death is usually sent to the registrar electronically, so you may not need to carry anything. Bring what you can find of the person's birth certificate, marriage or civil partnership certificate, National Insurance number and any pension details, plus your own photo identification.

Missing paperwork almost never prevents a registration. Go anyway.

## What you will be asked

Full name and any previous names, date and place of birth, occupation, usual address, marital status, and the names and occupations of both parents — including the mother's maiden surname. That last one catches people out; it is worth asking a relative beforehand.

## Remember

You may register a death at any registration office in Scotland. If it is easier to register near your own home than to travel to Haddington, you are entitled to do so.
