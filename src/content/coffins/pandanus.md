Pandanus is a coastal palm whose long leaves are cut, dried and woven flat. The result is quite different in character from willow or bamboo — a wider, softer weave with a muted grey-green tone.

The pandanus range is made by weavers accredited to the World Fair Trade Organisation, which sets standards on pay, working conditions and the absence of child labour. For families who care where a thing was made and by whom, that matters as much as the material.

Fully biodegradable. Suitable for burial, cremation and natural burial.
