Strong, light, made in the UK, and considerably less expensive than any other coffin. It is often chosen on cost, and there is nothing at all wrong with that — but plenty of families choose it for quite different reasons.

Because the surface takes pen, paint and glue, it can be written on and decorated. Grandchildren draw on it. People write letters on it. Families have covered one entirely in photographs. For a child's funeral in particular, being able to do something with your hands is sometimes the only thing that helps.

More than 70% recycled content, fully biodegradable, no plastics or metal. Suitable for burial, cremation and natural burial.

If you would like to decorate one, tell us early and we can get it to you in good time.
