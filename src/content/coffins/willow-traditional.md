Willow has been used for coffins in Britain for centuries, and it remains the most popular of the natural range. Each one is woven by hand, so no two are identical — the grain and colour vary with the crop.

The willow is grown as a fast-growing renewable crop and harvested annually from the same stools, which regrow. There is no plastic, no metal, no screws and no staples anywhere in the coffin.

Available in natural buff, golden brown and a darker chestnut. A cotton or calico lining is fitted as standard, and a name plate can be attached to the lid.

Suitable for burial, cremation and every natural burial ground we work with.
