The same construction as the traditional willow coffin, but with the willow grown in England — chiefly on the Somerset Levels, where it has been cultivated for generations — and woven in the UK.

Families choose it for the shorter supply chain and because the provenance can be stated plainly. It carries a slightly deeper colour than imported willow and a tighter weave.

Fully biodegradable, with no plastics, metals or fixings. Suitable for burial, cremation and natural burial.
