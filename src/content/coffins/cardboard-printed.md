The same UK-made recycled board as the plain cardboard coffin, printed across the whole surface.

Standard designs include woodgrain finishes for families who want the look of a traditional coffin at a fraction of the cost and weight, alongside landscapes, skies and floral patterns.

A photograph or image of your own can also be printed — a hillside they walked, a boat they sailed, a football crest. This takes longer to produce, so tell us as early as you can.

Fully biodegradable and suitable for burial, cremation and natural burial.
