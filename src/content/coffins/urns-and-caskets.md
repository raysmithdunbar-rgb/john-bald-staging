Ashes are returned in a plain container as standard, at no charge, and a great many families are content with that. If you would like something else, the range is wide.

**Scattering tubes** are designed to make scattering straightforward and less precarious than it sounds, with an opening that controls the flow. Available plain or printed.

**Biodegradable urns** are made from recycled paper, salt or plant fibre, and are intended to be buried or placed in water, where they break down completely. Some are made to hold a tree seed, so that something grows from the spot.

**Wooden and ceramic caskets** are for families who want to keep the ashes at home, or to inter them in a lair or columbarium.

**Keepsakes** hold a small quantity of ashes so that more than one person can have some — pendants, small caskets, and glass pieces with ashes set into them. See our [memorials page](/memorials/) for more.
