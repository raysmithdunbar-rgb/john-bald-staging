The banana plant fruits once and is then cut down. The leaf sheath, which would otherwise be discarded, is dried and woven — so the material is genuinely a by-product rather than a crop grown for the purpose.

The weave is coarser and more textured than willow, in a warm mid-brown that darkens slightly with handling. It is the most distinctive-looking coffin in the range.

Also made by Fair Trade accredited weavers. Fully biodegradable, and suitable for burial, cremation and natural burial.
