A shroud is the oldest form of burial there is, and for some families it is exactly right — nothing between the person and the ground but cloth.

Made from undyed British wool, with a rigid wooden board beneath for carrying and lowering, and integral fabric handles. It can be closed simply or laced, and flowers or letters can be tucked into the lacing.

Not every burial ground accepts shrouds, and they are not suitable for cremation. Natural burial grounds almost always permit them. We will check with the specific site before anything is arranged.
