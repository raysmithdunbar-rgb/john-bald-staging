Bamboo grows faster than almost any other plant used for this purpose — some species by nearly a metre a day — and regrows from the same root system without replanting. It is among the most genuinely renewable materials available.

The finished coffin is noticeably lighter in both colour and weight than willow, with a fine, even weave and a pale honey tone. Families often describe it as the least sombre of the range.

Fully biodegradable, with no plastics or metal fixings. Suitable for burial, cremation and natural burial.
