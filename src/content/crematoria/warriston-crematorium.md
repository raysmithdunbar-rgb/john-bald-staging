Warriston is Edinburgh's oldest crematorium — the first cremation took place here in 1929 — and it is privately operated by Edinburgh Crematorium Limited rather than by the council.

## What it is like

There are two chapels. The Lorimer Chapel is the older and more traditional of the two, with the character you would expect of a building of that period. The second chapel is smaller and plainer.

The grounds have had nearly a hundred years to establish, and it shows. For families who find modern crematoria impersonal, Warriston often suits better.

## Practical detail

Bookings are made through the crematorium directly. It sits close to the Water of Leith in the north of the city, which makes for a pleasant approach but a slightly awkward one in heavy traffic.

## Getting there from Dunbar

About 27 miles, typically 45 minutes to an hour depending on how far into the city centre the traffic reaches.
