Mortonhall is the City of Edinburgh Council's crematorium, on the south side of the city near the Braid Hills. It is the largest in the area and handles a substantial share of Edinburgh funerals.

## What it is like

There are two chapels of different sizes, which is genuinely useful — a large family funeral and a gathering of eight need different rooms, and Mortonhall can do both properly.

The grounds are extensive and mature, with a large garden of remembrance. It is a calm site despite its size.

## Practical detail

Bookings and enquiries go through the council's bereavement service. Because it is busy, popular times can book up further ahead than at smaller crematoria — worth knowing if the family has a date that matters.

Parking is adequate but can be tight when two services run close together.

## Getting there from Dunbar

Around 30 miles via the A1 and the city bypass, typically 45 minutes to an hour. The bypass is the variable — allow extra at peak times.
