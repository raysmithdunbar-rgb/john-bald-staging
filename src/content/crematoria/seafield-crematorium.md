Seafield sits on the coast in Leith and is operated by Edinburgh Crematorium Limited, the same company as Warriston.

## What it is like

The distinguishing feature is the adjoining Seafield Cemetery. Where a family already holds a lair there, ashes can be interred in the existing grave on the same site, which spares a second journey on a separate day.

The buildings are classically styled and the gardens well tended.

## Seafield Cemetery

The cemetery contains a number of Commonwealth War Graves Commission memorials to servicemen of both World Wars, individual and communal. The CWGC maintains online records if you are tracing someone.

## Practical detail

The administration office for both Seafield and Warriston is at The Lodge, Seafield Cemetery, Seafield Place. Bookings go through the crematorium directly rather than the council.

## Getting there from Dunbar

Around 25 miles via the A1, generally 40 to 50 minutes. Of the Edinburgh crematoria it is usually the least troublesome to reach from East Lothian, since it does not require crossing the city.
