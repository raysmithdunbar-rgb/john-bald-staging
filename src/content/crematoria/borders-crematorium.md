Borders Crematorium opened to give Borders families a local alternative to travelling to Edinburgh, which until then was the only option. For families in Berwickshire it is often the natural choice.

## What it is like

The setting is the thing people remember. It sits in open countryside beneath the Eildon Hills, within walking distance of Melrose and the Tweed, and the building was designed to sit into the landscape rather than dominate it.

The building is among the most environmentally efficient crematoria in Europe, which matters to families who have chosen a natural coffin or a green funeral and would rather the cremation itself was consistent with that.

## Practical detail

Services can be streamed for family unable to attend. The office is open Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm.

The gardens are open to visitors: April to September, 9am to 6pm on weekdays; October to March, 9am to 5pm; and 10am to 4.30pm at weekends and on public holidays.

## Memorials

A range of memorials is available in the Garden of Remembrance. Memorial sales are handled by the site office, with Saturday appointments by arrangement.

## Getting there from Dunbar

About 40 miles, roughly an hour, most easily via the A1 and then inland. Families coming from Berwickshire will find it considerably closer.
