East Lothian Crematorium sits just north of Old Craighall, a short distance from Musselburgh and about forty minutes from Dunbar. It opened relatively recently and, unlike most crematoria, does not feel like a municipal building.

[Houndwood](/directory/crematoria/houndwood-crematorium/), in Berwickshire, is nearer to Dunbar. But Houndwood stands on a hill in a 180-year-old church and is hard to reach for anyone with mobility difficulties, and where that matters East Lothian is the better choice. It is the one most families here use.

## What it is like

The chapel is bright and modern, with a great deal of natural light and views out to the gardens. Families who have found older crematoria oppressive often remark on the difference.

It is the only crematorium in Scotland with a full immersive audiovisual system. The walls of the chapel can carry imagery — a hillside, a coastline, a football ground, a favourite photograph at scale. It sounds like a gimmick written down, and for some families it is not the right thing at all, but used carefully it can transform a service. You are under no obligation to use it.

## Practical detail

Services are streamed for family who cannot travel, which is worth knowing if relatives are abroad. Recordings can usually be made available afterwards.

Parking is generous, and the approach is level, which matters if anyone in the family has difficulty walking.

## The gardens

The memorial gardens are extensive and well kept, with a range of memorial options including plaques, benches and rose bushes. Ashes can be interred or scattered here, and the gardens are open to visit outside service times.

## Getting there from Dunbar

Roughly 22 miles by the A1, around forty minutes in normal traffic. Allow longer at rush hour, when the approach to Musselburgh can be slow.
