Tranent Cemetery serves the town and the surrounding former mining villages.

## Lairs

Existing lairs may be reopened subject to depth; new lairs are purchased from East Lothian Council.

## Memorials

Council memorial regulations apply, with a permit required before erection.

Lairs, interments and memorial permissions are handled by East Lothian Council, not by the funeral director. We deal with them on your behalf as part of arranging the funeral.
