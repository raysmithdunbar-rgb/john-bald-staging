North Berwick Cemetery serves the town and the coastal villages westward toward Gullane and Aberlady.

## Lairs

Existing lairs can be reopened where depth allows; new lairs are purchased from East Lothian Council, subject to availability.

## Memorials

East Lothian Council's memorial regulations apply and a permit is required. Coastal exposure is worth a thought when choosing stone — a mason familiar with the site will advise.

Lairs, interments and memorial permissions are handled by East Lothian Council, not by the funeral director. We deal with them on your behalf as part of arranging the funeral.
