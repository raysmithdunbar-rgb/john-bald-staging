Haddington Cemetery serves the county town and the villages around it.

## Existing and new lairs

Both are arranged through East Lothian Council. Older sections contain many long-established family lairs; newer ground is available for new purchases, subject to availability.

## Memorials

The council's memorial regulations apply, and a permit is required before a headstone may be erected. Allow six to twelve months after burial for the ground to settle.

Lairs, interments and memorial permissions are handled by East Lothian Council, not by the funeral director. We deal with them on your behalf as part of arranging the funeral.
