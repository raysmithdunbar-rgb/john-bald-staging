Eyemouth Cemetery serves the town and the Berwickshire coastal villages. We arrange burials here regularly for families in the area.

## Lairs

Lairs and interments are arranged through Scottish Borders Council. Their fees and regulations differ from East Lothian's, so do not assume figures quoted for one apply to the other.

## Memorials

Scottish Borders Council publishes its own memorial regulations, and a permit is required. As with any coastal site, stone choice matters more than it does inland.

Lairs, interments and memorial permissions are handled by Scottish Borders Council, not by the funeral director. We deal with them on your behalf as part of arranging the funeral.
