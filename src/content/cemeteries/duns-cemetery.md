Duns Cemetery serves the town and the surrounding inland Berwickshire parishes.

## Lairs

Arranged through Scottish Borders Council, whose fees and regulations differ from East Lothian's.

## Memorials

Council memorial regulations apply and a permit is required before a stone may be erected.

Lairs, interments and memorial permissions are handled by Scottish Borders Council, not by the funeral director. We deal with them on your behalf as part of arranging the funeral.
