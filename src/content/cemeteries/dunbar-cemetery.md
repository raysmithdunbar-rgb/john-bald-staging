Dunbar Cemetery serves the town and the surrounding villages, and is where the majority of Dunbar families with an existing lair will find it.

## Existing lairs

Where a family already holds a lair, it can usually be reopened provided there is depth remaining. We will need the lair certificate, or the written permission of whoever holds the burial rights. If the certificate cannot be found, East Lothian Council holds its own records and we can almost always trace it — allow a little extra time.

## New lairs

New lairs are purchased from East Lothian Council. You are buying the exclusive right of burial for a set period rather than the ground itself, and that right can be passed on.

## Memorials

Headstones may normally be erected six to twelve months after burial, once the ground has settled. East Lothian Council publishes regulations covering permitted height, width and materials, and a memorial permit is required.

Lairs, interments and memorial permissions are handled by East Lothian Council, not by the funeral director. We deal with them on your behalf as part of arranging the funeral.
